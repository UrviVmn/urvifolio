Research Ideation Solution

From numbers to light you can feel

Interactive product design. Fitness Band turns numerical health data into a tangible, physical visualisation. A band records your run and a desk dock glows with it, so you don’t just track your effort, you feel it.

Timeline

Discover: Week 01 Brief & desk research, Week 02 Field research, Week 03 Synthesis & persona. Define & Ideate: Week 04 Concept directions, Week 05 System & interaction. Develop & Test: Week 06 Form & prototype, Week 07 Testing & review.

01 Brief

Physically inactive, desk-bound lifestyles are reducing daily movement in homes and workspaces. Activity has become optional rather than embedded. Design an embedded, interactive product system that cultivates habitual movement within homes and workspaces.

50% of users abandon new workout routines within a month. Under 45 min of structured movement a week, against WHO’s 150-minute target. 70% of young adults say fitness matters, yet remain inactive. Only 1 in 4 adults meet the recommended weekly physical activity levels (WHO, 2024).

What does being fit mean?

Fitness is subjective. It varies from person to person, shaped by individual needs, abilities, lifestyles and goals. The visible perception of fitness: looking fit, achieving physical benchmarks, completing a workout or challenge. The deeper meaning of fitness: individual, consistent, sustained.

“The Aim is, Not making everyone fitter against a single benchmark, but stopping inactivity from becoming the default way people live.”

02 Research

26.6M searches on wellness in India, yet most people never turn that curiosity into a habit. Interest is high. Action is low. 32% of the market is fitness apps; the space is crowded with screens.

Where current solutions fail

Awareness without action. Apps downloaded, not used. Motivation runs dry.

Why it’s urgent

Lifestyle diseases in the 20s. Stamina lost to screens. A mental-health risk.

We derived what people told us into themes

Personal Metrics, Lifestyle Dynamics, Spatial Assurances, Dopamine, Visible Validation, Confidence & Identity.

Derived insights

Every fitness journey runs through one loop: aspiration, blocker, facilitators, hold-up, validation. Gym anxiety, rigid tracking, broken routines, the valley of disappointment, immediate reinforcement.

Design tensions

Social workouts, fitness information and measuring fitness each pull between a helpful and a harmful side.

The problem

People struggle to stay physically active not due to lack of awareness, but because effort feels invisible, progress feels unrewarding and feedback feels discontinuous.

03 Persona

Adarsh, 23, Hyderabad, junior marketing executive in his first job. Newly independent young adults who need a cue to start. “I know I’m not fit, but I’m fit enough.”

How might we make effort feel as real as the result?

Concept

Shift what counts as progress: not weight lost, not calories burned, but the quality of effort.

04 System

One band, one dock, one app, each with a single job. The band records quietly. The dock lights bloom and data becomes presence. The app holds past sessions, patterns and insights.

How it works

One loop that compounds every day: idle, pick up, wear, move, return, display.

05 Interaction

Ten steps between the system and the user, from idle state to loop completion. System architecture: how data travels from wrist to table.

06 Data visualisation

Why light, and the rules every pattern follows. Activation = Movement × Duration × Intensity. Brightness shows effort level, colour shows activity type, pulse shows the transition between activities. Activity signatures for aerobic, weight training and field sports. Goal progression for a 10 km goal.

07 App

A quiet companion that explains the light: home with today’s glow, choose activity and set the goal, today’s summary. User flow and information architecture: four tabs, shallow depth, everything reachable in two taps.

The dock and the band

A soft, frosted LED body under a wave-textured cap. The band carries the same wave texture, so the two read as one family on the desk. Stay healthy. Stay fit.
