Connecting riders deeper with Bajaj

If bikes move people emotionally, apps should too. “Not just an app for the bike. From ride to rider.” Today, the connection ends when the ride does: the app is opened only when the bike is in use, not when the rider wants to feel connected.

Bajaj Ride Connect today

Across the category the utility is reliable. The problem is not what these apps do, it is what they leave out: they are designed as technical dashboards, demand attention while riding and ignore the emotional and social aspects of riding.

Design goal

Create a next-generation motorcycle app that reduces cognitive load and builds trust through context-aware, rider-centric experiences.

What exists today and the critical gaps

Same UI while riding and stationary, data-heavy and context-light, zero emotional engagement, no social or community layer, and no support for shared ownership.

Design process

Discover, define, develop, deliver. Instead of fixed personas, research focused on situational contexts: the urban commuter, the weekend tourer, the performance enthusiast and group riders.

Key observations from what riders told us

The ride doesn’t start when the engine starts: pre-ride, ride start, active riding, ride adaptation, destination, return and post-ride. Everything the rider told us points at one thing: the app talks at the wrong moment.

Insights from rider behaviour

Riding decisions are already cognitively heavy. Riders rely on phones despite knowing the risk. Small failures immediately reduce confidence in the entire system. Riders want systems that feel inevitable, not impressive.

Competitive scan

OEM motorcycle apps, EV ecosystems and standalone rider utilities. What riders actually value: accuracy, predictability, reassurance and low effort.

Opportunity areas

Passenger involvement, emotional connection, friend-based use and structured rental, on top of the baseline the app must keep.

Concepts

01 Cognitive Load Inference: the system reads rider stress, adapts in real time and shows less when focus matters. 02 Bajaj Ride Rings: everyday riding data turned into a living visual identity, earned by riding, not money. 03 Friends Biker Mode. 04 Bajaj ShareLink. 05 Technology integrated. 06 Language design. 07 UI.

Solution roadmap and final UI

Home, health, map, trips, service and profile, brought together in a calm, rider-centric app.
