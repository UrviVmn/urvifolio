Hi, I’m Urvi, a Product Designer pursuing a Bachelor of Design in Industrial Design at the National Institute of Design, Andhra Pradesh.
I turn curiosity into clarity, designing experiences that feel natural to use.
My approach is grounded in research-driven problem-solving, with a deep interest in human-centred design, cognitive ergonomics, interaction and user experience design.
Presently serving as Co-founder and Outreach Lead at Layover – A Design Dialogue, an industry-academia partnership program, where I manage session moderation, cross-functional coordination, and host events.
